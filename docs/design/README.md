# Design

This folder holds Dewfall's designs, made in Phase 1 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). It is planned to look like this:

- [brief.md](brief.md): the design brief.
- stitch/: DESIGN.md and screenshots of the direction picked in Stitch. These files record the Stitch exploration. Where they disagree with the brief, the brief wins.
- [stitch/directions/](stitch/directions/README.md): screenshots of the other directions tried in Stitch, and why podcast library was picked.
- claude-design/: the Claude Design export, with every screen and the three clickable prototypes: onboarding, inbox to player, and search inside videos.
- screens.md: the screen inventory, one line per screen with its file.

Anything not here yet arrives as the other Phase 1 tasks are done.
