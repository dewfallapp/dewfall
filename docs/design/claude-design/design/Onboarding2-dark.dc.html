Bring your channels

Import the channels you follow on YouTube from a Google Takeout file. Dewfall only needs the list of channels.

Getting the file

  1. 1Open takeout.google.com and choose only YouTube and YouTube Music.
  2. 2Tap All YouTube data included and tick only subscriptions.
  3. 3Create the export, then download the .zip file to this phone.

Google's labels can differ with your language.
