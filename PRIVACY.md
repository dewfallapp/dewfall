# Privacy

Dewfall collects no data about you.

- There are no accounts. You never sign in.
- There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
- Everything the app stores stays on your device. This includes your channels, watch history, downloaded videos and saved subtitles.
- If you export your data, it goes to a file you pick. The app sends it nowhere else.

## Network connections

Dewfall connects directly to YouTube to search, fetch channel and video details, download videos, stream them, and fetch subtitles. These requests go from your phone to YouTube with no Dewfall server in between. YouTube can see them, as it can for any app or browser that loads YouTube videos.

## Changes to this policy

Dewfall is in early development. This policy will be finalized before the first release, and any change will appear in this file.
